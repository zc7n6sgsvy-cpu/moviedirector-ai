# MovieDirector — Product Vibe & Identity (canonical)

## The badge we sell (end goal)

Users should feel true saying:

> **“I’m a movie director.”** / **“I’m a film director.”**

Not “content creator.” Not “I post clips.”  
**Director** is a psychological badge: craft, intent, authorship, pride.

Everything — feed, free tier, channels, language — either raises or cheapens that badge.

---

## Feed philosophy: revolving repertory, not death-timeline

Social apps kill posts with time decay. **Films should not die.**

### Repertory Rotation (live)

The catalog is a **living theater slate**:

| Signal | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Craft** | Ratings — good work stays worthy forever |
| **Still playing** | Recent watches/rewatches — if people still open it a year later, it **spins back onto the marquee** |
| **Second screening** | Under-seen work gets a fair pass (not buried by virals forever) |
| **Premiere** | Opening-week *spotlight only* — never a later penalty |
| **Daily salt** | Soft reshuffle so the shelf feels alive |

**Rule of thumb:**  
*Age does not bury you. Silence does. Attention keeps you in rotation.*

A year-old show that still gets watched belongs on the feed as much as a new drop.

## Monetization: passion first, paywall second (decision)

### Recommendation: **do NOT make subscription channels the default day-one identity**

| Path | What it optimizes | Risk to the badge |
|------|-------------------|-------------------|
| **Paywall-first channels** | Capital, hustle, “subscriber count” | Users think “I’m a creator selling access,” not “I’m a director.” Creativity tilts to extractive. |
| **Passion-first public repertory** | Pride, craft, fun, social proof | “I directed something people can watch.” Badge forms. |
| **Optional Director’s Club later** | Income when audience already cares | Monetize *after* identity — healthy. |

### Why this hijacks the vibe correctly

1. **Competing for capital early** changes the art. People optimize for paywall conversion, not a great cold open.
2. **Pure passion + public pride** is how you get “I’m a director” — the feed is the **theater lobby**, free to walk in.
3. **Channels still exist as optional infrastructure** (we already have the model):  
   - Free: premiere / pilot / best film on the public feed  
   - Optional paid channel: director’s cut, full season, BTS, early access — **only if they want it**  
   - Never force “stash everything behind paywall” as the default path

### When channels *are* a day-one benefit

Only as a **quiet power tool**, not the hero story:

- “When you’re ready, open a Director’s Club.”  
- Not “Build your subscription business.”  
- Not required for self-esteem loop.

**Self-esteem loop (must stay free):**  
Plan → direct → publish → see it in repertory → get a watch → feel like a director.

Money is chapter two for most people. Chapter one is the badge.

---

## Language rules (protect the badge)

| Use | Avoid |
|-----|--------|
| Film, episode, pilot, premiere | Content, post, drop (as primary) |
| Director, director’s cut | Creator (as primary identity) |
| Repertory, second screening, still playing | Trending (as the only goal) |
| Publish to the theater / feed | Grow your brand |

---

## Product shape (summary)

```
PUBLIC REPERTORY (free, pride, identity)
   ↑ watches keep films revolving forever
   │
OPTIONAL DIRECTOR’S CLUB (paid channel — later / opt-in)
   extras, seasons, deep cuts
```

**North star metric:**  
% of free users who publish once and return after someone watches their film — not MRR from day one.
